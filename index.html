The commercial context

More devices.
More complexity.
More need for visibility.

As connected products move from the lab into the field, reliability becomes a business issue—not just a debugging task. Product teams need to understand failures, connectivity, performance, and software behavior across entire fleets.

The phrase Device Observability sits at the intersection of those operational needs. This creates a credible commercial use case for a domain that precisely expresses the category.
